### Gandhi Hackathon 2026 · OpenLake × Meraz · Indian Institute of Technology Bhilai
*Theme: "The Abandoned Computer" · Presentation Round: Latent Format*

---

## 🌟 Overview & Premise

> *"You have discovered an abandoned computer running Windows 98. Your task is to bring this forgotten machine back to life by recreating its entire digital environment..."*

This project is an **ultra-elite, pixel-faithful recreation of Windows 98 Second Edition**, crafted specifically for the **Gandhi Hackathon 2026**. 

Rather than just another static skin, this is an **interactive digital time machine**. It resurrects the personal machine of **Vikram Sharma**, an engineering student at IIT in 1998–2001 who was deeply immersed in Y2K survival preparations, 56k dial-up internet culture, Napster MP3 sharing, and classified historical archives.

## 🚀 Key Features & Built-in Applications

### 1. 🖼️ MS Paint 98
* **16 Working Tools**: Pencil, Brush, Airbrush, Eraser, Line, Curve, Rectangle, Ellipse, Polygon, Rounded Rectangle, Text, Magnifier, Color Picker, Selection.
* **Flood Fill Bucket**: True BFS flood fill algorithm directly on the HTML5 Canvas.
* **28-Color Palette**: Dual primary/secondary color swatches (left-click / right-click).
* **Export**: Ability to save and download real `.png` artwork directly to disk.

### 2. 🎮 Doom 98: 3D Retro Raycasting Engine
* **Real-time 3D Graphics**: Custom DDA Raycasting Engine running at 60 FPS inside a classic Win98 window!
* **Textured & Shaded Walls**: Dynamic lighting and distance depth.
* **WASD / Arrow Controls**: Smooth movement, turning, and collision detection.
* **Interactive Gun**: Animated blaster sprite with muzzle flash and firing sound.
* **Live Mini-Map**: Dynamic radar tracking player coordinates and orientation.

### 3. ♠️ Solitaire (Klondike)
* **Playable Card Game**: 7 tableau columns, 4 foundation piles (Aces to Kings), Stock, and Waste pile.
* **Smart Auto-Move**: Double-click any face-up card to automatically move it to its suited foundation.
* **Score & Timer**: Tracks game time and moves.
* **Legendary Win Animation**: Bouncing card cascade across the green felt upon victory!

### 4. 💣 Minesweeper 98
* **Difficulty Levels**: Beginner (9x9, 10 mines) and Intermediate (16x16, 40 mines).
* **7-Segment LED Displays**: Live digital counters for remaining mines and elapsed timer.
* **Animated Smiley Face**: `🙂` Idle, `😮` Shocked on mouse press, `😵` Dead on bomb hit, `😎` Sunglasses on win.
* **Recursive Blank Expansion**: Auto-reveals connected blank areas.

### 5. 📻 Winamp 2.x Media Player
* *"It really whips the llama's ass!"*
* **Real-time FFT Audio Spectrum**: Animated 20-bar visualizer (Green → Yellow → Red).
* **Preloaded 90s Chiptune Playlist**:
  1. *Eminem - Without Me (Chiptune 2002)*
  2. *Darude - Sandstorm (Techno Synth 1999)*
  3. *Doom - At Doom's Gate (E1M1 FM Synth)*
  4. *Windows 98 Ambient Chill Chime*
* **Interactive Controls**: Play, Pause, Stop, Previous, Next, Seek, and Volume control.

### 6. 🌐 Internet Explorer 5.0 & 56k Dial-Up
* **1998 Google Beta**: Authentic Stanford-era layout with functioning queries.
* **GeoCities Retro Personal Page**: Complete with dancing baby, flame graphics, hit counter, and signable guestbook.
* **Yahoo! 1998 Portal**: Classic hierarchical directory categories.
* **Dial-Up Networking**: Realistic procedural synthesis of the 56k V.90 modem negotiation squeals, tones, and handshakes!

### 7. 📟 MS-DOS Prompt
* **Full Shell Interpreter**:
  * `DIR` – Lists realistic directories and file sizes.
  * `CD` – Traverse directory tree (`CD 'My Documents'`, `CD ..`, `CD \`).
  * `TYPE` – Inspect contents of files.
  * `MATRIX` – Real-time falling green digital rain!
  * `HACK` – Hollywood cyber-intrusion simulation.
  * `LATENT` / `SAMAY` – Special Easter egg tailored for the Latent hackathon judging panel!
  * `FORMAT C:` – Interactive scary prompt with safe recovery.

### 8. 📁 Virtual Filesystem & "The Abandoned Computer" Lore
* `C:\My Documents\1998\`: `world_cup_1998.jpg` (France 3-0 Brazil), `titanic_1997.jpg`.
* `C:\My Documents\1999\`: `diary_december_1999_y2k.txt` (Vikram's frantic diary on stocking Maggi noodles for the Millennium Bug).
* `C:\My Documents\2001\`: `most_wanted_list.jpg`, `news_clipping_2001.jpg`, `9-11-attack-planning.pdf`.
* `C:\My Documents\Secret_Vault\`: `LATENT_JUDGES_BRIBE_PROTOCOL.txt`.

### 9. 📎 Clippy: The Hackathon Assistant
* Interactive animated assistant sitting on the desktop.
* Taps the glass, blinks, and offers witty commentary customized for the **Latent** format judging round!

### 10. 📺 CRT Monitor Shader & Bootloader
* **Award Modular BIOS POST**: Memory test, Quantum Fireball HDD detection.
* **Windows 98 Boot Splash**: Authentic moving gradient loading bar.
* **CRT Overlay**: Scanlines, curvature, phosphor bloom, toggleable from the system tray!
* **Blue Screen of Death (BSOD)**: Triggerable via `crash` command or icon, with automatic warm reboot!

## 🔊 Pure Procedural Web Audio Engine
**Zero external MP3 dependencies!**
Every sound is procedurally synthesized via the Web Audio API in real time:
- Windows 98 Startup Chord Progression (F# Major Triad Warm Pad)
- Windows Error Chord & Asterisk Dings
- 56k Dial-Up Modem Carrier & Handshake Frequencies
- Minesweeper Explosions & Retro Arcade Bleeps
- 8-bit / Chiptune Melodies

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

- **Vanilla HTML5 / Modern Modular CSS3 / ES6+ JavaScript**: Zero heavyweight framework overhead; instant 60 FPS performance.
- **Custom Window Management System**: Dragging, boundary detection, edge resizing, minimize, maximize, restore, z-index layering, and taskbar sync.
- **100% Crisp Vector Graphics**: All Windows 98 icons are pure SVG data URIs, ensuring razor-sharp rendering on 4K retina displays.
